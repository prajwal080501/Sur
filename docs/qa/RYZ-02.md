# RYZ 02 Tanpura verification

The current implementation is a synthesized development instrument, as permitted by the handover
specification until owned or CC0 samples are available. Its pure pitch plan is unit-tested for Pa,
Ma, Ni, octave placement, and shared fine tuning. The browser engine applies Sa and tuning changes
on the next pluck and uses gain ramps to avoid oscillator start and stop clicks.

Sample-loop continuity and the ten-minute offline-render criterion remain pending until the licensed
sampler replaces this development implementation.
